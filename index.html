Roll SKU cost — weighted average for this period

SKURollsTotal CostAvg £/roll
Averages are weighted by actual production volume — total cost across every day in this period, divided by total rolls or kg — not a plain average of each day's rate. £/roll converts each day's cost using that day's own locked exchange rate, then sums, so a rate change partway through the period is reflected correctly rather than applied retroactively. Batches without a matching SKU (Custom) are grouped together under "Custom".

        
Copies one row per SKU, all 6 columns — Product Name, backing 1, backing 2, density, Ex-Factory Price (GBP), Effective Date — tab-separated, ready to paste directly into "Product description" in the same order. Backing 1/2 are copied exactly as they're stored in the app's SKU master (e.g. "NONWOVEN YELLOW") — no relabeling. Effective Date is formatted to match your sheet's existing style (e.g. "01-Jan-26"). Defaults to the £/roll figure for whatever period is selected above, so this works for a full month or a mid-month price change equally. Names are copied exactly as they appear in this app's SKU master — since some don't yet match your Google Sheet's naming, rename either side to line them up before pasting, or paste and fix afterward. "Custom" batches (no matched SKU) are skipped, since they have no product name to write against.

Electricity, wood & currency rates

      

      

        

          
          

            
            
          

        

      

      

      
The GBP rate is only used to show a £ figure alongside ₹ — it never affects the actual ₹ costing. Each day's entry locks in whatever this rate was when you saved it, so old dates' £ figures stay fixed even if you update the rate later; only new saves pick up a changed rate. "Refresh live rate" pulls the current rate from a public exchange service — review it and click Save settings to apply it.

Electricity and Wood rates below are effective-dated — add a new rate with the date it takes effect, and every day is costed using whatever rate was actually in force on that date, past or future. No existing data is retroactively affected unless you edit a past rate's value directly.

    


    

      
Labor rates — ₹ per person, applied to the headcount entered in Daily Log

      

      
Daily Log takes a headcount per stage instead of a total ₹ figure — the app multiplies by whichever rate was effective on that entry's date. Add a new rate below with the date wages actually changed — days before that date keep using the old rate, automatically.

    

    

      
Monthly overheads — spread across production days automatically

      

      
Allocation basis: shared foaming labor, electricity, and daily overhead split between Sheets and Underlay by weight of blocks each line drew from foaming — raw material is charged directly per line, not allocated. Within a line, the allocated share is spread evenly per kg across every SKU produced that day. These are also effective-dated — add a new figure with the date it takes effect (e.g. a rent increase from a specific month) and each day is costed using whatever was in force then.

    


    

      
Raw materials & opening stock — consumption is now costed by the weighted-average ledger, not a typed rate

      

        

          
MaterialFallback rate ₹/kgOpening stock (kg)Opening stock rate ₹/kg

          
        

      

      

        
        
      

      
Opening stock sets the ledger's starting point — the quantity and average cost of each material already sitting in the yard before you started logging purchases here. Fallback rate is only used if a material is consumed with zero purchase history and no opening stock (so the ledger has nothing to base a weighted average on). Once purchases are logged in the Daily Log, the ledger takes over automatically — see the Inventory tab for current stock and cost.

Trim/wastage entered in Daily Log is treated as reclaimed material: it's valued at the current ledger rate of whichever material is named "Rebonded" (matched automatically by name — keep that word in the material's name for this to work), credited back against that line's raw material cost, and added to that material's stock at the same rate — so reused trim isn't double-counted as a pure loss.

Roll lamination & packing rates

      

      

      

      
These rates are effective-dated. When you pick a roll SKU in Daily Log (or import via Excel), the app looks up whichever rate was in force on that entry's date — not today's live rate — so backdating an old day's log always uses the pricing that was actually correct then.
